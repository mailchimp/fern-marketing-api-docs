---
title: Added Survey Reporting
tags: ["Marketing API"]
---

We've added various endpoints to pull reports for a survey. You can start by viewing [all surveys](https://mailchimp.com/developer/marketing/api/reporting-surveys/) or export high-level results from the [Survey Questions](https://mailchimp.com/developer/marketing/api/reporting-surveys-questions/). You can also pull [individual Survey Responses](https://mailchimp.com/developer/marketing/api/reporting-surveys-responses/get-survey-response/) to get more detail.

Many of you have asked for a way to pull survey results via the Mailchimp Marketing API, and now there are various endpoints to help you do this.
