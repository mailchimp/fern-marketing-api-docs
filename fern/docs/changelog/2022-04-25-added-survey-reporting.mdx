---
title: Added Survey Reporting
tags: ["Marketing API"]
---

We've added various endpoints to pull reports for a survey. You can start by viewing [all surveys](/marketing/api/reporting/list-surveys) or export high-level results from the [Survey Questions](/marketing/api/reporting/list-survey-questions). You can also pull [individual Survey Responses](/marketing/api/reporting/get-survey-respons) to get more detail.

Many of you have asked for a way to pull survey results via the Mailchimp Marketing API, and now there are various endpoints to help you do this.
