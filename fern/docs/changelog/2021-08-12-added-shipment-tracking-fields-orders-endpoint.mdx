---
title: Added shipment-tracking fields to orders endpoint
tags: ["Marketing API"]
---

The [`/ecommerce/orders`](/marketing/api/ecommerce/list-store-orders) endpoint now accepts `tracking_number`, `tracking_carrier`, and `tracking_url`** **fields for an order’s shipment-tracking details.

This addition is in preparation for a future feature to notify shoppers of the tracking number, carrier (e.g. USPS, FedEx), and tracking URL for their orders. You can populate these fields in advance so they are ready to use when classic automations fully support them.
