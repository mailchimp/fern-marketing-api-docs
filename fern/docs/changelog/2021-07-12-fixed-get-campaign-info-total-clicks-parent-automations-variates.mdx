---
title: Fixed Get Campaign Info to return total clicks for parent automations and variates
tags: ["Marketing API"]
---

We fixed a bug in the `report_summary` section of the [individual campaigns endpoint](/marketing/api/campaigns/get) for parent automation and variate campaigns. Previously, we reported 0 total clicks for those campaign types; now we will report the sum of clicks between the child campaigns.

This falls in line with the expected behavior elsewhere in the `report_summary` section for other stats (like opens).
