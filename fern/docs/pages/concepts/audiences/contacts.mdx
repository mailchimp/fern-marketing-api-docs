## The basics

A contact is a person in an audience, identified by their email address. In the
API, contacts are addressed by `subscriber_hash` — the MD5 hash of the
lowercase email address — so `GET /3.0/lists/{list_id}/members/{subscriber_hash}`
retrieves one contact from one audience.

The same person in two audiences is two contacts. Audiences don't share contact
records.

## Contact status and channel subscription

Two separate fields describe a contact's reachability, and they operate at different levels.

**Contact-level `status`** (`active` or `archived`) describes the contact overall. It is not a subscription status. An active contact is one that exists and is not archived; it says nothing about whether you can send to them on any channel.

**Channel-level `effective_subscription_status`** describes whether a contact can be reached on a specific channel—email or SMS. This field is read-only. You cannot set it directly; Mailchimp computes it from three inputs:

1. **Audience opt-in configuration** — whether the audience uses single opt-in or double opt-in.
2. **Marketing consent** — the value you supply when creating or updating a contact: `unknown`, `consented`, `confirmed`, or `denied`.
3. **Deliverability** — whether the contact is reachable on that channel. Mailchimp manages this internally; for example, if a phone number becomes unregistered, the deliverability status changes and the effective subscription status may change with it, even if the consent value you supplied has not changed.

The consent value you send is a statement about what the contact actually did—it is not a direct instruction to subscribe or unsubscribe them. Mailchimp maps that value, together with the audience's opt-in setting and deliverability, to the resulting subscription status.

### SMS consent and the double opt-in flow

For SMS specifically, the consent value you send determines whether Mailchimp initiates the double opt-in confirmation or treats the contact as already confirmed.

**On a double opt-in audience:**

- Send `consented` when the contact has interacted with you but has not yet confirmed via SMS. Mailchimp puts the contact in `pending` and sends the confirmation message. The contact's effective subscription status becomes `subscribed` or `unsubscribed` depending on whether they confirm.
- Send `confirmed` when you already have verified consent from a prior system and want to skip the double opt-in flow. The contact is subscribed directly without receiving a confirmation message.

**On a single opt-in audience:**

- Send `confirmed` to subscribe the contact directly.
- `consented` is not supported for SMS single opt-in.

<Warning>
`denied` for SMS is not yet supported in this beta. Unsubscribed SMS contacts must be managed manually. Do not send `denied` for an SMS channel; the value is not accepted.
</Warning>

For the complete mapping of consent value to effective subscription status across all channel and opt-in combinations, see [Audiences endpoints (beta)](/marketing/api-concepts/audiences-endpoints-beta#contact-status-vs-channel-level-subscription-status). For a practical walkthrough of adding contacts with consent values, see [Import and export contacts](/marketing/build/quickstarts/import-and-export-contacts).

## Tags

[Tags](https://mailchimp.com/help/getting-started-tags/) are labels that help
organize your contacts. You define what they mean: bring your own targeting and
organization strategy into Mailchimp rather than adopting one.

You could use tags to:

- Structure campaigns for specific audiences, tagging contacts with terms like "Holiday Send" or "Influencer"
- Directly map tags from your CRM onto tags in your Mailchimp audience
- Translate behaviors—for example, a contact who's spent $1,000 in your store—into tags that can be used in future campaigns

Tags attach to individual contacts, at
`/3.0/lists/{list_id}/members/{subscriber_hash}/tags`. There is no
audience-level tag resource; to find which contacts carry a tag, search with
`GET /3.0/lists/{list_id}/tag-search` or build a
[segment](/marketing/concepts/audiences/segments) that filters on it.

For a walkthrough of tagging contacts with the API, see
[Organize contacts with tags](/marketing/build/quickstarts/organize-contacts-with-tags).

## Merge field values

Each contact carries values for the merge fields its audience defines. The
field has to exist on the audience before you can set a value on a contact —
see [Merge fields](/marketing/concepts/audiences/merge-fields).
