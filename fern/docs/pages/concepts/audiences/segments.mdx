title: Segments
description: Subsets of contacts in an audience, defined by their attributes, activity, or tags.

## The basics

A segment is a named subset of the contacts in an audience. Mailchimp
distinguishes four kinds of segment:

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Regular segment | You define conditions, such as signup date, campaign engagement, merge field values, or e-commerce activity, under a single `any` or `all` match. Its membership is every contact who currently matches them, recalculated as your contact data changes. The Segments endpoints report it as `type: saved`. |
| Advanced segment | Like a regular segment, but its conditions sit in groups that combine `any` and `all` logic. It has its own endpoints and definition format; see [Advanced segments](/marketing/concepts/audiences/advanced-segments). Requires a Mailchimp plan that includes advanced segmentation. |
| Google Analytics segment | Built by Mailchimp from Google Analytics data. Read-only: you can list, read, count, and snapshot it through the [advanced segments endpoints](/marketing/concepts/audiences/advanced-segments#read-only-segments), but you can't change its membership rules. |
| Static segment | You add contacts to it explicitly, with no conditions. Membership only changes when you add or remove someone. |

**Static segments are also tags.** When you [tag a contact](/marketing/concepts/audiences/contacts#tags), Mailchimp creates or reuses a static segment behind the scenes. A static segment and a tag are the same underlying object, just reached through different features. See [Contacts](/marketing/concepts/audiences/contacts#tags) for tagging.

## Segment conditions

A regular segment's conditions are grouped under a match type, `any` or `all`,
which determines whether a contact needs to satisfy just one condition or
every condition to belong to the segment. Each condition targets a specific
kind of data—a merge field, a campaign interaction, a purchase, a location—and
each kind of data has its own condition shape, documented in
[Alternative schemas](/marketing/api-concepts/alternative-schemas).

<Note>
The Segments endpoints described here support regular segments: a single match
type applied across up to 10 conditions. Advanced segments, which combine
and/or logic across groups of conditions, have their own endpoints and
definition format; see [Advanced segments](/marketing/concepts/audiences/advanced-segments).
An advanced segment can contain up to 100 conditions in up to 2 levels of
groups, and working with one requires a Mailchimp plan that includes advanced
segmentation.
</Note>

## Working with segments through the API

The [Segments endpoints](/marketing/api/lists/list-segments) let you create,
update, and delete segments, and read back which contacts currently belong to
one with [List members in segment](/marketing/api/lists/list-segment-members).
For a regular segment, that membership list reflects the conditions evaluated
against your contacts right now, not a snapshot from when the segment was
created.
