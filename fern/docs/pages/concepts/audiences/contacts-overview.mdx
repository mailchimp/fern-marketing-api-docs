Manage contacts within a Mailchimp audience. A contact represents a person,
separate from the email and SMS channels they can be reached on; each
channel's subscription status is computed from the audience's opt-in setting,
the marketing consent you supply, and deliverability.

<Note>
Your Mailchimp audience is where you store and manage all of your omni-channel contacts.
